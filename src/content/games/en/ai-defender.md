## About the game

AI Defender is a browser game about defending a digital core. Viruses arrive in waves, and agents attack automatically. Leveling up pauses combat so you can choose an upgrade or deploy another agent.

The campaign contains 53 battles. As you progress, agents unlock with beams, chain lightning, and slowing effects. Between battles, you can upgrade agents and their equipment.

<a id="как-играть" class="translation-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></a>

## How to play

Choose a starting agent and keep an eye on the core's health. Drag an agent into an empty slot with a mouse or a finger to reposition it. Save an unfinished battle from the exit menu and resume it from the command center.
